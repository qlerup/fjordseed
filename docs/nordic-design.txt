NORDISK — FjordSeed theme design, 31 October 2026

Aim
A distinct, historically informed Viking Age interface, preserving all application
controls and workflows. This is a modern design inspired by archaeological evidence,
not a claim that a website or an imagined harbour is a historical reconstruction.

Composition
A compact top navigation and status/title row lead into a modern dark workspace.
No photographic banner or presentation-style hero. The generated harbour is only
faintly visible at the unused lower right edge; data surfaces are opaque. On mobile
that decorative image is hidden. Colour, line icons, bronze actions and a small
runic detail carry the historical character without replacing modern app controls.
On desktop download metrics and the queue share one row. Download/upload rates
lead the overview, with the measured-traffic route diagram beside them. On mobile
rates appear above the diagram. Dense torrent rows retain all existing actions.

Historical basis and deliberate modern choices
- Younger futhark, 16 characters, is the relevant Viking Age alphabet. The six runes
  in the title ornament are its opening sequence: f u th a r k (not a translation of
  FjordSeed, and not a magical inscription). The ornament is hidden from screen readers.
  https://www.vikingeskibsmuseet.dk/runer
  https://nors.ku.dk/forskning/navneforskning/runologi/
- Runic writing had no dedicated digits; numbers were written as words. Operational
  metrics therefore retain modern numerals and units, with tabular numerals for clarity.
  https://www.raa.se/kulturarv/runor-och-runstenar/runskolan/skriva-och-lasa-runor/
- The palette draws on ochre, red/brown earth pigments, charcoal and light natural
  materials. It is an interpretation, not a pigment or colour reconstruction.
  https://www.vikingeskibsmuseet.dk/frontend/Dokumenter/Farver_DK_udskriftsark_2.pdf
- Viking ornament includes stylised animals and interlace. This informed the
  research; the final modern interface keeps decoration minimal. The geometric
  ship emblem is original artwork, not an authentic museum artefact.
  https://vikingar.historiska.se/objects.php?e=no&l=en&showcase=ec28e1a8-e260-4f92-bac2-d15e88af1360
- The illustration takes inspiration from clinker-built coastal trading vessels,
  including the Danish oak Skuldelev 3, rather than presenting every ship as a warship.
  https://www.vikingeskibsmuseet.dk/en/visit-the-museum/exhibitions/the-five-viking-ships/skuldelev-3
- Hall, vessel and craft pictograms are modern UI artwork. Segoe UI headings and compact tabular metrics keep the interface operational;
  modern UI typography is not presented as a Viking typeface. Danish labels,
  accessible names, form controls and numbers retain their original meaning.

Motion
A faint drift at the edge of the workspace obeys background pause, hidden-tab, modal
and reduced-motion settings. Traffic uses existing measured rates and packet timing,
but draws a quiet single route per direction without the old starfield and glow.
The VPN indicator no longer scales on every arriving packet. Graphs cross-fade
instead of morphing their curves. Existing chart timing, hover pause, keyboard
navigation and data remain unchanged. Seed-status motion is a quiet opacity pulse.

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: ultra-wide photographic masthead background for a carefully designed Viking Age inspired productivity app. Create a realistic archaeological reconstruction of a quiet Scandinavian coastal trading harbour around 1000 CE at dawn. Small clinker-built oak coastal cargo vessel inspired by Skuldelev 3 moored in the right third, natural brown timber hull, single mast with furled rectangular wool sail in faded ochre and muted iron red, simple rope rigging, no oversized dragon head, no shields. Low shoreline at right with two modest timber buildings with turf or thatch roofs. Weathered timber pier at far right. Calm pewter blue sea, low mist and wooded rocky coastline receding into distance, warm muted dawn light behind cloud. Composition: horizon at lower middle, huge quiet empty sky and water on LEFT and centre for white application heading text. Photorealistic landscape / museum reconstruction, convincing natural materials, dignified and quiet. Palette: charcoal, grey seawater, oak, flax, ochre, muted iron red. Wide 16:9 panorama suitable to crop into a shallow header. No people, no fighting, no medieval castles, no horned helmets, no magical symbols, no runic inscriptions, no modern objects, no text or logo. This is an illustrative reconstruction not an exact depiction of any excavated harbour.

Workspace revision, 31 October 2026
Overview now has a dedicated left traffic/VPN rail with a vertical network route.
Tracker totals run across the top of the main workspace; graph selectors form a
right inspector column. Downloads use a separate status/queue rail, searchable
worklist and new action/progress composition. Settings use a left navigation rail.
These layouts collapse independently on tablet/mobile; shared control behavior stays intact.
Desktop navigation (>1100px) hangs as a dark oak sign from two visible ropes.
It sways continuously by 0.14 degrees, including on hover, as requested. Mobile
retains the compact app bar. The motion preference and reduced-motion setting
both disable the sway. nordic-sign.svg is original vector artwork, not a photograph.

The desktop sign is content-sized and centered, with no empty full-width extension.
Braided rope frames (original nordic-rope.svg) edge the major panels and dialogs;
small controls retain clear conventional borders. Mobile frames are thinner.

Harbour edition, 31 October 2026
The final direction is a modern illustrated Viking workshop, not a photographic
banner. nordic-chart.svg is a lightweight original sea chart with coast contours,
a decorative modern compass, route marks and abstract rune forms. This is modern
UI artwork, not a historical map or an inscription with a claimed translation.
Ink blue replaces the solid green background. Major surfaces use smoked oak,
iron-grey interiors, bronze details and dark brown braided hemp frames.
The compact hanging desktop sign stays. Its suspension ropes are now dark brown.

The traffic canvas is an independent harbour scene: two longships carry download
traffic inward and upload outward. Vessel motion follows positive measured rates;
a stopped connection has no sailing motion. The old node diagram remains only in
the other themes. Each torrent has separate inbound/outbound animated vessel
lanes; Downloads totals also show live vessels. Existing measured progress bars,
labels, percentages, seeding targets, buttons and APIs retain their behavior.
Reduced motion and the saved animation preference freeze these decorative layers.
nordic-vessel.svg, nordic-chart.svg and the updated preview are original vector art.
A photographic background was explored with built-in imagegen but was not selected
after the user's preference for a modern illustrated direction. No new generated
raster is referenced or shipped by this edition.
